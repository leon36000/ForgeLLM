# ADR-NNNN: Concise decision title

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded | rejected
- **Successor:** ADR-NNNN (required when Status is superseded)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Owners:** named human owner and reviewing role
- **Related tasks/claims:** identifiers

## Context

Describe the concrete problem, constraints, current evidence and why a decision is needed now.

## Decision

State one precise decision, including scope and versioned interfaces affected.

## Alternatives considered

For each serious alternative, state benefits, costs, risks and evidence.

## Consequences

List positive effects, negative effects, migration cost, lock-in, operational impact and testing implications.

## Safety and correctness invariants

State ownership, concurrency, numerical, error-handling and compatibility invariants introduced or preserved.

## Evidence required for review

Define the experiment, benchmark, prototype or review condition that could confirm or overturn this decision.

## Reversal condition

State measurable conditions that trigger reconsideration.
